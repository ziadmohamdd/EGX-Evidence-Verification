# Egyptian Investment Guidance

Investment guidance for individual investors in Egyptian listed companies. Financial evidence, investor circumstances, and existing holdings provide the context for understanding proposed investment changes and their risks.

## Language

### Investors and portfolios

**Investment Guide**:
An application that helps individual investors understand investment choices and their associated risks. Its guidance is not a guarantee of investment returns.

**Investor Profile**:
A description of an investor's goals, investment horizon, liquidity needs, willingness to accept losses, and financial ability to withstand losses.

**Investment Horizon**:
The period before an investor expects to need the invested money.

**Current Portfolio**:
The investor's existing holdings and available cash at a stated time.

**Proposed Portfolio**:
The holdings and cash allocation that would result if proposed investment changes were followed. It is not a record of executed trades.

### Guidance and scenarios

**Recommended Plan**:
An investment proposal consistent with the investor's assessed goals and constraints, subject to stated assumptions and uncertainty.

**User-Directed Simulation**:
A hypothetical analysis of a strategy requested by the investor, including a strategy identified as unsuitable. It is not an endorsement or a prediction of guaranteed outcomes.

**Sell Recommendation**:
A proposed reduction of an existing holding. It does not involve an unowned security or a quantity greater than the investor holds.

### Financial evidence

**Source Value**:
A financial value as it appears in an original document, table, or image, together with its surrounding context. Its presence in a source does not establish that its interpretation is correct.

**Verified Financial Figure**:
A financial value whose digits, unit or currency, reporting period, entity, and accounting meaning have been checked against supporting evidence. Verification does not independently establish that an issuer's disclosure is true.

**Ambiguous Financial Figure**:
A source value with an unresolved interpretation, such as uncertain digits, scale, reporting period, or accounting basis.

**Information Cutoff**:
The latest time at which information is considered available for a particular investment analysis. It distinguishes historical analysis from analysis intended to inform a new investment decision.
